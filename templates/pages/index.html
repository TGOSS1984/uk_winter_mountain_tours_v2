Regions to Explore

Mountains of Wales

The Brecon Beacons & Snowdonia National park contain some of the UK's classic routes, including tryfan & for those with a head for heights, Crib Goch, the most exciting route up to Snowdon!

Explore
Mountains of Scotland

The Scottish Mountains can be some of the most wild in the UK. From the Caingorms to Glencoe & the Isle of Skye, there is something for everybody

Explore
Mountains of the Lake District

Explore the Wainwirght's, with classic routes & various grades of scrambling. From the classic ridges of Helvellyn to the Scafell range including Englands highest peak

Explore
Peak District & Yorkshire Dales

Explore the Peak District or Yorkshire Dales. Tackle Kinder Scout & the Mam Tor ridge, or go big on the Dales’ Three Peaks: Ingleborough, Whernside & Pen-y-ghent
